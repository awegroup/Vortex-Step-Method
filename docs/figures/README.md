# Figures: consistent lifting-line coupling

Generated by `make_pr_figures.py` (run from the repository root, a few
minutes). They document the changes that align the solver with Gaunaa, Li &
Pirrung (TORQUE 2026, "From thin airfoil theory to finite wings"), Li, Gaunaa,
Pirrung & Lønbæk (TORQUE 2026, "Relaxation limits and artificial viscosity")
and Gaunaa, Sørensen & Li (2024, spanwise-flow viscous correction).
`v3_polars_before_fixes.json` holds the V3 polars of the solver before these
changes; the current code cannot reproduce them.

## 01 V3 polars before and after
TUDELFT V3 kite, CAD geometry, CFD+NeuralFoil polars, 50 panels, 2.82 m/s,
against RANS (Poland 2025, Vire 2022) and wind-tunnel data (Poland 2025).
Dashed black: the solver before the changes. Blue: after the changes with the
3/4-chord force directions (the old default). Red: with quarter-chord
directions (`is_aoa_corrected=True`, the paper's LL-Gaunaa, now the default),
dotted without the attached-trailed force. The quarter-chord variant sits on the RANS drag in the
attached range and in sideslip; the 3/4-chord variants overshoot it. The
wind tunnel carries bridle drag and a lower Reynolds number.

## 02 Panel frame, drag direction and viscous-correction angle
Top left: panel axes at the V3 left tip. Blue is the old frame (chord along
the rib, normal from the leading-edge step), red the new span-perpendicular
frame, black the span. Top right: the old frame's chord-to-span skew per
panel and the angle by which the old profile-drag direction (global span axis
crossed with the lift direction) missed the local flow, up to 108 deg at the
tips. Bottom: the spanwise-flow angle beta fed to the Gaunaa 2024 viscous
correction, old (a geometry-only number that never saw the flow) versus new
(from the relative velocity), at 0 and 10 deg sideslip.

## 03 Trefftz-plane check
Inviscid wings, on-blade induced drag divided by the Trefftz-plane induced
drag on the same circulation (`results["drag_induced_trefftz"]`). Quarter-
chord directions with the attached-trailed force reproduce the far-wake drag
exactly on straight wings, within 2% on the elliptic wing, 6% low on the
30 deg swept wing and 7-14% low on the V3. The 3/4-chord directions overshoot
by 20-40%. Without the attached-trailed force the V3 is 11-28% low.

## 04 Attached-trailed vortex force on the V3
Kutta-Joukowski force on the chordwise legs between the bound vortex and the
trailing edge (Gaunaa, Li & Pirrung 2026, Sec. 3), inviscid V3 at 10 deg.
Left: per-boundary force vectors. Right: their vertical and streamwise
components; largest over the curved outboard region, zero at mid-span where
the legs are aligned with the flow. Total 18 N of 760 N lift.

## 05 Adaptive relaxation factor
Left: iterations of the base loop on the V3 with the old fixed 0.01 and with
the default derived from the Li et al. 2026 stability limit (Eq. 11 in
per-panel form), for VSM and LLT. Right: the measured stability limit (from
the fixed-point Jacobian) over the paper's bound on rectangular wings: the
bound is exact for LLT and about half for VSM, whose 3/4-chord control point
sees twice the trailing-vortex induction; the solver halves it for VSM.

## 06 Wake direction under a yaw rate
Top view of the wake origins with the old single wake direction (mean
freestream before the rotational term) and the new per-ring direction (each
panel's own apparent velocity). Identical in uniform inflow; under body rates
the wake follows the local inflow. A yaw rate mostly changes the local speed,
so the direction differences are small even at the exaggerated 1 rad/s.

## 07 Perpendicular chordwise legs, tried and rejected
The paper aligns the attached legs with the local chord, which its CP1 defines
perpendicular to the quarter-chord line; the solver keeps them along the input
ribs. On the V3 tips a perpendicular leg only meets the trailing edge far
inboard, and on a swept wing with streamwise ribs the legs cross neighbouring
panels and their control points (NaN). Against RANS the V3 mean errors are
0.020 in CL and 1.5% in CD with rib legs, 0.031 / 4.7% with fixed-length
perpendicular legs and 0.057 / 9.4% with legs to the trailing edge.
